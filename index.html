Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Everlost: Dark Adventure (“the game”) is developed by Fatih Murre (“we”).

Data we collect

None. The game works fully offline. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no third-party services, and it does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (the last checkpoint, unlocked chapters) and your settings (sound, language) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for small haptic bumps during play. Nothing else.

Children

The game does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
